About me

Practical support, thoughtful thinking.

I am a business support professional with a Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy and hands-on experience in sales supervision, lead generation, client communication, social media growth and AI-assisted content creation. I also have experience participating in and engaging with Web3 communities across crypto, Web2 and Web3 spaces, mainly on Discord and X.
